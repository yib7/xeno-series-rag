# Xeno Series RAG - Gold Question Set

**200 questions** (25 per game across all 8 Xeno titles), each with a documented correct
answer grounded in the indexed wiki corpus and linked to its source page on xenoserieswiki.org.
This is an answer key for evaluating retrieval + generation quality; the machine-readable version
is [`gold_questions.json`](gold_questions.json).

Game codes: XG = Xenogears; XS1/XS2/XS3 = Xenosaga Episode I/II/III; XC1/XC2/XC3 = Xenoblade
Chronicles 1/2/3; XCX = Xenoblade Chronicles X. Categories span characters, enemy/boss stats,
art/attack values, collectible locations, quests, world/lore, items, and mechanics.

## Xenogears (XG)

**XG-01 - character**
- Q: Who is the main protagonist of Xenogears?
- A: Fei Fong Wong, the main protagonist of Xenogears and pilot of the Gear Weltall.
- Source: [Fei](https://www.xenoserieswiki.org/wiki/Fei)

**XG-02 - character**
- Q: Who is Citan Uzuki?
- A: Citan Uzuki, also known as Hyuga Ricdeau, is a playable character in Xenogears - a doctor in Lahan Village who becomes Fei's mentor and ally.
- Source: [Citan](https://www.xenoserieswiki.org/wiki/Citan)

**XG-03 - character**
- Q: Who is Bart in Xenogears?
- A: Bartholomew Fatima, nicknamed Bart, is a playable character - the exiled crown prince of Aveh and the leader of the Sand Pirates.
- Source: [Bart](https://www.xenoserieswiki.org/wiki/Bart)

**XG-04 - character**
- Q: Who is Elly, and what is her full name?
- A: Elhaym Van Houten, nicknamed Elly, is a playable character and the main heroine of Xenogears, an accomplished soldier within Gebler.
- Source: [Elly](https://www.xenoserieswiki.org/wiki/Elly)

**XG-05 - character**
- Q: What is Emeralda in Xenogears?
- A: Emeralda is a playable character, a mysterious girl made from a Nanomachine colony (aged 3,917) who has a connection to Fei and Elly.

**XG-06 - character**

**XG-07 - character**

## Xenosaga Episode I (XS1)

**XS1-01 - character**
- Q: Who is Shion Uzuki?
- A: Shion Uzuki is a playable character and one of the protagonists of the Xenosaga series, the Chief Engineer at the KOS-MOS Project in Vector Industries' First R&D Division.
- Source: [Shion](https://www.xenoserieswiki.org/wiki/Shion)

**XS1-02 - character**
- Q: Who is KOS-MOS?
- A: KOS-MOS (Kosmos Obey Strategic Multiple Operation Systems) is an anti-Gnosis battle android developed by Vector Industries' KOS-MOS Project, and a central character of the series.
- Source: [KOS-MOS](https://www.xenoserieswiki.org/wiki/KOS-MOS)

**XS1-03 - character**
- Q: Who is chaos in Xenosaga?
- A: chaos is a playable character, a mysterious boy whose past is unknown (later revealed to be connected to Yeshua and an incarnation of Anima).
- Source: [Chaos](https://www.xenoserieswiki.org/wiki/Chaos)

**XS1-04 - character**
- Q: Who is Ziggy (Ziggurat 8)?
- A: Ziggurat 8, nicknamed Ziggy and originally Jan Sauer, is a playable combat cyborg resurrected by Ziggurat Industries and assigned to protect MOMO. In life he was a Federation police officer.
- Source: [Ziggy](https://www.xenoserieswiki.org/wiki/Ziggy)

**XS1-05 - character**
- Q: What is MOMO in Xenosaga?
- A: MOMO Mizrahi is a playable Realian, the 100-Series prototype, and pilots the E.S. Zebulun.
- Source: [MOMO](https://www.xenoserieswiki.org/wiki/MOMO)

**XS1-06 - character**
- Q: Who is Jr. (Gaignun Kukai Jr.)?
- A: Gaignun Kukai Jr., originally Rubedo, is a playable protagonist - co-director of the Kukai Foundation and commander of the Durandal, with "666" engraved on his left palm.
- Source: [Jr.](https://www.xenoserieswiki.org/wiki/Jr.)

**XS1-07 - character**
- Q: Who is the primary antagonist of Xenosaga Episode I, Albedo?
- A: Albedo Piazzolla, the White Testament, is the primary antagonist of Episode I - a U.R.T.V. affiliated with the U-TIC Organization who pursues the Y-Data and pilots the E.S. Simeon.
- Source: [Albedo Piazzolla](https://www.xenoserieswiki.org/wiki/Albedo_Piazzolla)

**XS1-08 - character**

**XS1-09 - character**

**XS1-10 - character**

**XS1-11 - world**
- Q: What are the Gnosis?
- A: The Gnosis (also known as the Pilgrimage Fleet) are an alien race that first appeared en masse in T.C. 4753 during the Miltian Conflict and have threatened humanity since.

**XS1-12 - world**

- A: A.G.W.S. stands for Anti-Gnosis Weapon System - manned mobile weapons about 4-5 meters tall, deployed to combat the Gnosis.
- Source: [A.G.W.S.](https://www.xenoserieswiki.org/wiki/A.G.W.S.)

**XS1-13 - world**
- Q: What are Realians?
- A: Realians are a species of inorganic synthetic humanoids modeled after humans.
- Source: [Realian](https://www.xenoserieswiki.org/wiki/Realian)

**XS1-14 - world**

- A: U.R.T.V.s (U-DO Retro Virus) are genetically modified humans created by Dmitri Yuriev; there are 669 of them, and their sole purpose is to destroy U-DO.

## Xenoblade Chronicles X (XCX)

**XCX-01 - character**
- Q: Who is the player character in Xenoblade Chronicles X?
- A: Cross (often called Rook), the fully customizable custom character recruited by Elma to join BLADE; they are always present on the player's team.
- Source: [Cross](https://www.xenoserieswiki.org/wiki/Cross)

**XCX-02 - character**
- Q: Who is Elma in Xenoblade Chronicles X?
- A: Elma is a main playable character and the central protagonist, a BLADE team leader (Reclaimers) whose responsibility is to recover the Lifehold. She is secretly a disguised xenoform.
- Source: [Elma](https://www.xenoserieswiki.org/wiki/Elma)

**XCX-03 - character**
- Q: Who is Lin Lee Koo?
- A: Lin is a playable character, a 13-year-old genius mechanic and a BLADE Outfitter whose skills extend to Skell tech.
- Source: [Lin](https://www.xenoserieswiki.org/wiki/Lin)

**XCX-04 - character**
- Q: Who is Tatsu, and is he playable?
- A: Tatsu is a Nopon party member who is NOT playable. A member of the Dodonga Caravan once kept by the Prone as food, he was taken in by Lin and serves as comic relief and a source of intel about Mira.
- Source: [Tatsu](https://www.xenoserieswiki.org/wiki/Tatsu)

**XCX-05 - character**
- Q: Who is Lao Huang?
- A: Lao is a playable character, a marksman who leads Team Belisarda of the Pathfinders, working to find the Lifehold Core. His wife and daughter were left behind on Earth.
- Source: [Lao](https://www.xenoserieswiki.org/wiki/Lao)

**XCX-06 - character**
- Q: Who is the commander of BLADE in Xenoblade Chronicles X?
- A: Jack Vandham, the commander of BLADE and the main source of story-related assignments in New Los Angeles. He previously led the Outfitters and was Chief Engineer of the White Whale.
- Source: [Vandham (XCX)](https://www.xenoserieswiki.org/wiki/Vandham_(XCX))

**XCX-07 - character**
- Q: Who is Doug in Xenoblade Chronicles X?
- A: Doug Barrett is a playable character, a BLADE Harrier and daring Skell pilot who often serves as Lin's test pilot.
- Source: [Doug](https://www.xenoserieswiki.org/wiki/Doug)

**XCX-08 - character**
- Q: Who is the Murderess?
- A: Sharon Effinger, known as the Murderess, is a recruitable BLADE Curator with remarkable field skills but a greed so intense she will betray even fellow BLADEs.
- Source: [Murderess](https://www.xenoserieswiki.org/wiki/Murderess)

**XCX-09 - world**
- Q: What does BLADE stand for in Xenoblade Chronicles X?
- A: BLADE stands for Builders of a Legacy After the Destruction of Earth. It is the central human organization, formed by Kentaro Nagi after the White Whale reached Mira, tasked with recovering the Lifeholds and defending New Los Angeles.
- Source: [BLADE](https://www.xenoserieswiki.org/wiki/BLADE)

**XCX-10 - world**
- Q: What is New Los Angeles?
- A: New Los Angeles (NLA) is the city in Xenoblade Chronicles X and the game's only major settlement. It was the Habitat Unit of the White Whale, ejected and cushioned by a protective gel as it crash-landed on Mira.
- Source: [New Los Angeles](https://www.xenoserieswiki.org/wiki/New_Los_Angeles)

**XCX-11 - world**
- Q: What is the planet Mira?
- A: Mira is the main setting of Xenoblade Chronicles X, a planet of five major continents that is mostly ocean and is inhabited by a wide variety of life known as indigens.
- Source: [Mira](https://www.xenoserieswiki.org/wiki/Mira)

**XCX-12 - world**
- Q: What are Skells in Xenoblade Chronicles X?
- A: Skells (Japanese: Doll) are mechs that can be piloted by the party and are used for both traversal and combat.
- Source: [Skell](https://www.xenoserieswiki.org/wiki/Skell)

**XCX-13 - mechanic**
- Q: How do Soul Voices work in combat?
- A: Soul Voices are battle cues: a party member calls out a command (e.g. "Move in and do some damage!"), and obeying it with the matching art deals about 50% more damage (or 100% from behind the enemy), and can also heal.
- Source: [Soul Voice](https://www.xenoserieswiki.org/wiki/Soul_Voice)
