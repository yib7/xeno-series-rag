     ANSWER: In *Xenoblade Chronicles*, Gem Crafting is the process of converting Ether Crystals or cylinders into Gems, which can be slotted into equipment to provide stat boosts or special effects.

The process is divided into three phases:

### 1. Setup
*   **Materials:** You must select between two and eight items (crystals or cylinders) of the same rank. You can include any number of different qualities. As items are added, the strengths of matching qualities combine. You cannot add more items if any quality reaches 100% strength.
*   **Personnel:** You must select two party members to operate the Ether Furnace: a "Shooter" and an "Engineer." The number of turns available for crafting depends on the affinity between these two characters.
*   **Location:** Gem Crafting can be performed by speaking to the Gem Man in Colony 9's Commercial District. Alternatively, after completing the quest "The Gem Man's Invention," you receive the Mobile Furnace key item, which allows you to craft gems from the menu at any time.

### 2. Crafting
Crafting occurs over several turns. During each turn, the Engineer selects a flame setting, which the Shooter then applies:
*   **Strong Flame:** Significantly increases the strength of a single quality.
*   **Medium Flame:** Provides a small increase to the strength of all qualities.
*   **Gentle Flame:** Adds to the Cylinder Gauge instead of affecting qualities.

This phase is largely random, though you may trigger a "Fever" (granting extra flame shots) or receive "Party Support" (allowing a non-participating character to assist). You have the option to speed up the process.

### 3. Collection
*   **Gem Creation:** Any quality that reaches 100% strength or higher is turned into a gem.
*   **Cylinders:** If a quality fails to reach 100% strength, it can be saved as a cylinder for future use, provided the Cylinder Gauge was filled during the crafting process.

**Note on *Future Connected*:** Gem crafting is not available in the *Future Connected* mode. In that version, gems are obtained directly from ether deposits, and enemies do not drop crystals or cylinders.

     ANSWER: In *Xenoblade Chronicles 3*, classes serve as a fundamental battle mechanic that determines a party member's role, weapon, and Arts. The system functions through the following mechanics:

### Party Members and Class Assignment
*   **Initial Classes:** The six main party members begin the game with a fixed initial class.
*   **Switching:** As the game progresses, the six main characters can switch to any class they have unlocked.
*   **Heroes:** By recruiting "Heroes" as seventh party members, the main party members can typically gain access to that Hero's class.
*   **Future Redeemed:** In the *Future Redeemed* expansion, classes are exclusive to each character and cannot be learned or shared by others.

### Ranking and Mastery
*   **Rank Progression:** Most main-game classes have a maximum of 20 ranks. Characters track these ranks individually.
*   **Earning CP:** Players increase their rank by earning Class Points (CP) while actively using a class or by spending Silver Nopon Coins. If a class has reached its rank cap (such as 10 or 20), the character will no longer earn CP for that class.
*   **Mastery:** As a character ranks up a class, they gain higher stat modifiers and more effective Arts and skills. Certain Arts and skills can be "mastered," allowing them to be used while the character is equipped with a different class.

*   **Special Exceptions:** Certain cutscenes may force a character into their default outfit, and flashbacks typically only show default outfits.

*   **Class Succession Points vs. CP:** Class Succession Points are used specifically to unlock new classes, whereas Class Points (CP) are used exclusively to increase the rank of a class a character has already unlocked.

     ANSWER: In *Xenoblade Chronicles X*, the Overdrive system is a combat mechanic that provides various offensive and utility benefits. Its functionality and mechanics are as follows:

### Activation and Cost
*   **TP Cost:** Standard activation costs 3000 TP. However, using the "Overdrive: Gain TP" effect can reduce the effective cost. With five counts of this effect and a max-level "Grand Procession," the net TP cost to activate Overdrive can be reduced to zero.
*   **Sequential Use:** Activating Overdrive twice in succession allows for the stacking of certain effects. If a fighter has 6000 TP, they can double the impact of "Phantom Counter" or "Overdrive Count Up" by doing this.
*   **Lockouts:** If you activate Cockpit Time while *not* in Overdrive, there is a 30-second lockout before it can trigger again. This lockout does not occur if Cockpit Time is activated while Overdrive is already active.

### Overdrive Count and Duration
*   **Count:** The Overdrive "count" determines the effectiveness of your combat actions. Higher counts allow purple Arts to fill TP faster, green and blue Arts to increase the Overdrive duration more efficiently, and offensive Arts to deal more damage.
*   **Boosting the Count:** The count can be increased at the start of Overdrive using specific skills or augments:
    *   **Phantom Counter:** Increases count by 4–12 based on the skill level.
    *   **Overdrive Count Up:** Increases count by 1–20 based on the total value of augments equipped. These stack; for example, two max-level augments will boost the count by 40.
*   **Extending Duration:** While green and blue Arts provide timer extensions (up to 5 seconds per green Art and 10 seconds per blue Art at max count), skills like "Extend Overdrive" or "Hellhound" are useful in the early game for building count when standard durations are too short.

### Skell-Specific Mechanics
*   **Cockpit Time:** While fighting in a Skell, Overdrive provides an additional +200% cooldown boost to all Arts during Cockpit Time. This stacks with other cooldown boosts, resulting in different reduction percentages for various Skell models (e.g., 75% for Formula, 80% for Ares, and 67% for others).
*   **Specialized Benefits:** Certain Skells receive unique bonuses during Overdrive, such as boosted Ranged Attack (Inferno, Mastema, Verus, and Amdusias) or the ability to use rare reflect augments (Verus).
*   **Shockwave Damage:** In certain modes, spamming Overdrive activation deals damage to all enemies in combat via an "Overdrive shockwave."
